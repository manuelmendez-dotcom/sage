# One-page CSM brief

Default to 450–650 words in the conversation. No new presentation. If a file is
requested, save Markdown or use available document tools for the chosen format.
For a literal Word/PDF one-pager, render and verify one readable page; word count
alone is not proof. Use the user's language and concrete, conversational phrasing.

Use this flexible shape; remove unsupported or redundant rows:

**[Customer] — Renewal / Discovery Brief**
Period: [verified] · Products: [reported owned products] · Prepared: [date]
Meeting/renewal: [known date, otherwise omit] · CSM preparation

**The account story**
Two or three sentences the CSM could say aloud: strongest measured outcome,
main unresolved challenge and decision to explore. Do not claim Zendesk caused
the observed improvement. Old priorities remain questions.

**VALUE ALREADY REALISED**

| Evidence | What it means for the conversation |
| --- | --- |
| [Result + matching YoY change + source locator] | [Plain interpretation, preserving period/aggregation] |
| [Comparable industry peers + source locator] | [Relative position, not a promised target] |

Normally two or three meaningful wins. If progress is unproven, call this “Current
value and position.” Do not force a positive story, financial savings or treat
activity as value. Briefly identify unavailable YoY/peer comparisons.

**FOCUS AREAS & NEXT VALUE MOVES**

Up to three numbered priorities, each in two or three compact sentences:

**[Outcome-focused priority].** [Account signal, period, source.] Propose [bounded
action] using **[verified Zendesk capability]** to [expected operational benefit].
**Measure:** [outcome/quality check; baseline if known]. **Check:** [material plan,
readiness, activation or EAP condition].

Every suggestion must tie to a signal. Prefer owned-product adoption; label
expansion/EAP. Put operational constraints beside affected priorities, or as one
short watch item. No causal or guaranteed improvement claims.

**DISCOVERY QUESTIONS**
Two or three questions that could change priority/feasibility. Do not ask for facts
already in the report. Explore whether dated objectives remain important, what
changed in the affected workflow, or readiness for a scoped test.

**PROPOSED NEXT STEP**
Choose one priority on the call, agree a customer owner, confirm baseline and
success criteria, and set a check-in. Never present proposed decisions as agreed.

## Separate CSM source notes

Keep exact slide/page sources, formulas, review coverage and current product URLs
in a linked appendix or separate CSM note. Every headline/recommendation maps to
those notes; short source IDs may remain in the brief. Keep the one-page narrative
free of extraction methodology and source-graph detail.

Internal decks, private relationship evidence and commercial comments stay in CSM
preparation. For customer-ready copy remove private lineage, use public product
links, and retain all caveats that affect the conclusion.
