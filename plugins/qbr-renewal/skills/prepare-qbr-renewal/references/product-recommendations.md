## Form a recommendation

Connect **signal → proposed action → named capability → expected operational
benefit → measure → eligibility/readiness check**. Normally select two or three
priorities, fewer if evidence is thin. Prefer improving what the customer owns.
Discuss expansion only where the account evidence supports it and label it.

For every selected action record:

- Exact account evidence and current countertrend/context.
- A bounded proposed action, and why it fits without claiming causality.
- Named Zendesk capability and current product source/terminology.
- Eligibility: **owned/included and verified**, **eligibility to confirm**, or
  **additional purchase / EAP** as supported. “Included” needs plan and applicable
  documentation; subscription ownership does not prove activation.
- Outcome measure, known baseline and quality check where relevant. Unknown
  targets, timing and owners are proposed/to agree, never commitments.
- One discovery question that could change the recommendation.

Admin Copilot, Knowledge Copilot, Analyst Copilot, Automation Potential, AI Agents,
Copilot, reporting, knowledge management, workflow automation, QA and WFM are
research leads, not a static approved catalogue. Verify current names and
availability; select by account need, never force one of each.

Rising resolution time, for example, supports investigating complex-case age,
handoffs and case mix. Owned AI Agents may justify assessing a bounded routine-
inquiry use case, subject to activation, knowledge, escalation and quality checks.
It does not prove routine volume caused delay or that AI will reduce complex-case
resolution time. Keep these measurement populations separate.

Keep an internal coverage stamp: access time, root identity/count/revision,
indexed text/notes gaps, mapped sections, opened branches, public sources,
conflicts, failures and deliberate exclusions. Remove internal master links and
private lineage from customer-facing copy.
